About Maras Group

A dynamic, privately owned and operated South Australian company, broadly considered one of the “pace-setters” in the South Australian market.

For over 40 years the group has been at the forefront of commercial and retail property investment and development, with a lengthy and impressive track record of quality and sustainable developments, underpinned by high calibre, long term occupiers.

We develop with a view to holding our properties for the long term, giving our tenants (our partners) the confidence to build strong, healthy relationships with us. And we stay heavily committed to investment in South Australia.
